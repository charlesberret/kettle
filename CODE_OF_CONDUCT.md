# Code of conduct

Kettle welcomes thoughtful participation from people of different backgrounds
and experience levels.

Be respectful, assume good faith, critique the work rather than the person, and
make room for questions. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language,
deliberate intimidation, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in
project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
needed to protect a constructive environment.

Kettle is currently a solo-maintained project with no other contributors to
have conduct concerns about. Once the project has collaborators who interact
with each other, this policy will name a formal contact for private conduct
reports, handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies to project repositories, issue trackers, review threads,
and public events where someone represents Kettle.
