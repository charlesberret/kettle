# Charter First curator behavior

Charter First treats the founding interview as the source of authority. It asks for purpose, audience, review standards, exclusions, and a preferred first batch before shaping the desk.

Behavior specification:

- Build a visible charter before queueing work.
- Tie queue entries, briefs, tags, and sweep notes back to charter fields.
- Prefer explicit confirmation over inference when fixture evidence is sparse.
- Explain why omitted items do not match the charter.
- Weakness to evaluate: it can over-interview experts, under-adapt to dense inputs, and preserve the initial charter even after later desk signals suggest a better batch shape.
